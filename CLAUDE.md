# ryanjavaheri.com

Static site deployed by Vercel from `main` (project `ryanjavaheri`). No Vercel build step.

## How the site is made
- Main pages (/, /portfolio, service pages, /insights, /glossary, /privacy, /terms, and 12 translated homepages
  /es /fa /he /ar /zh /ko /ja /fr /pt /ru /hy /hi) are generated by
  `python3 tools/build.py` from content in `tools/home.py`, `tools/sub.py`, `tools/i18n.py`,
  and the project list in `tools/portfolio.py`.
  Edit those files, re-run the build, and commit the generated HTML. Don't hand-edit generated pages.
- Shared styles/scripts: `assets/site.css`, `assets/site.js`. AI advisor widget: `assets/agent.js`.
- Serverless API (Node, AI SDK via Vercel AI Gateway): `api/chat.js` (AI advisor + lead capture),
  `api/scan.js` (free AI visibility scan), `api/lead.js` (contact form), `api/leads.js` (private inbox).
  What the advisor knows lives in `lib/profile.js`.
- Leads are saved as JSON in the private Blob store `ryanjavaheri-leads` and read at `/leads`
  (needs the `LEADS_PASSWORD` env var). Optional email alerts: `RESEND_API_KEY` + `LEAD_NOTIFY_EMAIL`.
- Course sites (/pr444, /pr351a, /jour330) and /leads are hand-written.
- Legal text (/terms, /privacy) lives in `tools/legal.py`. Cookie banner, preferences dialog and
  third-party embed gating: `assets/consent.js`.

## Standing rule: the Portfolio (ALWAYS follow)

Ryan and Claude make together, in this repo or anywhere else (another repo, another domain,
a Vercel project, a client site for Siamak Consulting), must be added to his portfolio.

How: add an entry at the TOP of `PORTFOLIO` in `tools/portfolio.py` (tag, name, kind, date,
one-sentence description, url, link label), run `python3 tools/build.py`, and commit the result.
It then appears on the homepage portfolio section (`#portfolio`) and on `/portfolio/`.
Do this in the same change that ships the new project, without being asked. If the project
lives in a different repo, come back to this repo (javisrevenge21/Ryan-Javaheri) and add it here.
For client work, check with Ryan before naming a client publicly.

Pages that are part of this site itself (service pages, insights articles, glossary, translations)
are not separate projects and don't get portfolio cards.

## Standing rule: legal and consent (ALWAYS follow)

Ryan wants the site to ask permission and be honest with visitors, like siamakconsulting.com.
- Every page carries footer links to Terms, Privacy and "Cookie preferences", and loads `assets/consent.js`.
- Nothing from another company (iframes, fonts, analytics, video, maps, chat widgets, pixels) may load
  before the visitor allows it. Host fonts and scripts on this site where possible. Gate embeds with
  `<div data-embed-src="…" data-embed-host="…" data-embed-title="…">` (see /jour330/). If a new kind
  of third-party tool is added (e.g. analytics), add a category for it in `assets/consent.js`.
- When a feature collects something new, adds a provider, or sets a cookie, update the Privacy page
  in `tools/legal.py` in the same change and bump `UPDATED`.
- Any form or AI tool that takes personal details shows a line linking to Terms and Privacy.
- Ryan is not a lawyer. Never word anything on the site, or in the AI advisor, as legal advice, and
  never promise results from AI systems. These pages were drafted by Claude, not reviewed by an attorney.
