AI intake agents that answer 24/7 and hand you the lead.

An assistant that knows your business, answers questions in the visitor's language, and collects what you need to follow up, on your website or your phone line.

Try one now: the advisor at the bottom right of this page is a live example. Ask it anything, press Talk to use your voice, or leave your details and watch it hand them to me.

What an intake agent does

  • Answers questions about your services, process, location and hours, using only facts you approve.
  • Takes a proper intake: name, contact details, what they need, timeline, and anything else you ask for.
  • Sends you the lead with the full conversation, to a private inbox, email or your CRM.
  • Speaks their language. It replies in whatever language the visitor uses.
  • Knows its limits. It never invents prices or gives legal, medical or financial advice. It escalates to you.

Web, voice and phone

Website chat: a chat widget like mine, styled to your brand. Voice on the web: visitors can just talk to it. Phone line: the same agent can answer a real phone number, take messages and book callbacks, so after-hours calls stop going to voicemail.

Automation around it

An agent is most useful when the next step happens automatically: the lead lands in your CRM, the right person gets a text, the customer gets a confirmation email, and a follow-up goes out if nobody replies. I connect those pieces with the tools you already use.

Frequently asked questions

Will the agent pretend to be a human?

No. My agents say they're an AI assistant for your business. Honesty builds trust, and many jurisdictions expect it.

What does it cost to run?

Running costs are usually small and depend on volume. You get a clear quote covering the build and monthly running costs before we start.

Can it work for a law firm without giving legal advice?

Yes. It answers general questions about the firm, collects the facts of the inquiry, and routes it to an attorney. It's instructed never to give legal advice.
