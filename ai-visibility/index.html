AI Visibility (LLMO): become the business AI recommends.

When a customer asks an AI assistant who to hire, it names a few businesses. I make sure yours is one of them.

In short: LLMO (Large Language Model Optimization) makes your business easy for AI models to find, understand, verify and recommend. It works alongside SEO, not instead of it.

What's included

  • AI visibility audit. What ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity say about you today, and what they say about your competitors.
  • Entity clarity. One consistent description of who you are, what you do and where, on your site and across the web.
  • Structured data. Schema.org markup (Organization, Person, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ) so machines read your facts without guessing.
  • Answer-ready content. Pages that answer the exact questions customers ask AI, written to be quoted.
  • llms.txt and crawl access. A plain summary file for AI systems, and making sure AI crawlers can actually read your site.
  • Citations and trust. Listings, profiles, reviews and mentions that let models confirm you're real and reputable.
  • Monthly re-checks. Track how AI answers change and keep improving.

How it's different from SEO

SEO competes for a ranked list of links. AI assistants usually give one synthesized answer and name a handful of sources. That rewards clarity, consistency and trust more than keyword volume. Good LLMO usually improves SEO too, because both depend on clear, credible content.

Who it's for

Law firms, local service businesses, professional practices, e-commerce brands and personal brands that win when someone asks "who's the best ___ near me?"

I learned this approach working with Siamak Consulting and apply the same principles here, starting with the B.U.I.L.D. method: Baseline, Understand, Implement, Launch agents, Develop.

Frequently asked questions

How long does LLMO take to work?

Some changes, like fixing structured data and site clarity, can show up as soon as AI tools re-read your site. Building trust signals and citations compounds over months. I re-check AI answers monthly so you can see progress.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?

No one honestly can. AI answers vary by question, user and model. What I can do is remove the reasons AI skips you and build the signals models rely on, then measure the change.

Do I still need SEO?

Yes. Search engines still drive traffic and many AI tools use search results as sources. LLMO and SEO reinforce each other.
