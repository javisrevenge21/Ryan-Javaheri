The AI visibility glossary.

16 terms, explained simply.

LLMO (Large Language Model Optimization)
Making a business easy for AI assistants to understand, verify and recommend.
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
Optimizing content to appear in AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Largely overlaps with LLMO.
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
Structuring content so search and voice assistants can lift it as a direct answer.
SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
Improving how a site ranks in traditional search results.
AI visibility
How often, and how accurately, AI assistants mention your business when people ask relevant questions.
Entity
A distinct thing a machine can identify, such as your business, a person or a place. AI visibility starts with being a clear entity.
Structured data / Schema
Code (usually JSON-LD using schema.org vocabulary) that labels facts on a page, like business name, address, services and FAQs.
llms.txt
A plain-text file at the root of a website that summarizes it for AI systems, similar in spirit to robots.txt.
Citation
A mention of your business on another site, such as a directory, review site or article, that helps confirm you're real and reputable.
Grounding
When an AI model bases its answer on specific retrieved sources rather than memory alone.
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
A technique where an AI system retrieves documents and uses them to write its answer.
AI agent
Software that uses an AI model to take actions toward a goal, such as answering a customer and saving their details.
Intake agent
An AI agent that greets inquiries, answers basic questions and collects information for a business to follow up.
Voice agent
An AI agent that talks by voice, on a website or a phone line.
Hallucination
When an AI model states something false with confidence. Good agents are restricted to approved facts to avoid it.
B.U.I.L.D. method
Ryan Javaheri's five-step process: Baseline, Understand, Implement, Launch agents, Develop.
