Why ChatGPT doesn't mention your business

The five most common reasons, and the fix for each.

Short answer: AI assistants recommend businesses they can clearly understand and confirm. If your information is vague, inconsistent, hidden or uncorroborated, they pick someone else.

1. Your site doesn't say what you do plainly

Clever taglines don't help a model. "Building tomorrow, today" tells it nothing. Fix: lead with a literal sentence: "Family law firm in Pasadena handling divorce, custody and support."

2. Your details don't match across the web

Different business names, old addresses or mismatched services on listings make models unsure you're one entity. Fix: audit and align every listing and profile.

3. Nobody else vouches for you

Models lean on corroboration: reviews, directories, press, associations, partner sites. Fix: build a steady set of trustworthy mentions and reviews.

4. Your answers are buried

If the answer to "do you handle X in Y?" lives on page six of a PDF, it might as well not exist. Fix: FAQ and service pages with short, direct answers, marked up with FAQ schema.

5. AI tools can't read your site

Heavy scripts, blocked crawlers or content inside images can hide you. Fix: make sure key content is real text, crawlers aren't blocked, and you publish a sitemap and llms.txt.
