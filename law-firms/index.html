Law firms: be the firm AI recommends, and never miss an inquiry.

AI visibility and intake for firms, backed by a USC education in business law and public relations, plus hands-on recruiting and PR support.

Get named when clients ask AI

People increasingly ask AI assistants things like "best employment lawyer in Los Angeles." I make sure your practice areas, attorneys, locations and credentials are clear and consistent, marked up with structured data, and backed by answer-ready pages, so models can recommend you with confidence.

24/7 intake without legal advice

An AI intake agent answers general questions about your firm, collects the facts of the matter and contact details, flags urgency, and routes it to the right attorney. It is instructed never to give legal advice. Read more in AI intake agents for law firms.

Talent and recruiting support

  • Support lateral attorney recruitment and connect with top legal talent
  • Market intelligence on firm culture, compensation and growth trajectories
  • Recruitment and retention strategies that build lasting pipelines
  • Early-talent relationships with promising law students

PR and positioning

Positioning, messaging and media relations that attract the right clients and candidates, and protect your reputation when it matters. My PR training at USC Annenberg shapes how I write every page and agent script.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a lawyer?

No. I'm a pre-law student studying public relations and business law at USC. I don't give legal advice; I help firms with visibility, intake, recruiting and communications.

Is AI intake compliant with attorney advertising rules?

Rules vary by state. The agent is set up to identify itself as AI, avoid legal advice and avoid promising outcomes, and your firm reviews and approves its knowledge and wording before launch.
