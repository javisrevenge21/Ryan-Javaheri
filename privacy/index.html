Privacy

Short and plain: what this site collects and why.

This site is run by Ryan Javaheri, Los Angeles, California. Contact: ryanjavaheri21@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Details you choose to share through the contact form, the free AI scan, or the AI advisor: such as your name, email, phone, business and what you need.
  • AI advisor conversations. When you leave contact details with the AI advisor, the conversation is saved with your lead so I can follow up properly.
  • Basic technical logs from the hosting provider (Vercel), used to keep the site running and secure.

How it's used

Only to reply to you and to provide the services you asked about. I don't sell your information or use it for unrelated advertising.

Service providers

The site is hosted on Vercel, and leads are stored in private Vercel storage. AI advisor and scan messages are processed by an AI model provider through Vercel AI Gateway to generate replies. Please don't share passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information in the chat.

Your choices

Email me any time to see, correct or delete your information.
