Privacy Policy

Plain language: what this site collects, why, and how to control it.

In short: no ads, no analytics trackers, no selling your data. I only receive what you choose to send me, and I use it to reply to you. Embedded content from other companies loads only if you allow it.

Last updated November 14, 2026.

Who runs this site

ryanjavaheri.com is run by Ryan Javaheri, an individual in Los Angeles, California. I am the person responsible for the information described here. Contact: ryanjavaheri21@gmail.com.

What this site collects

  • Contact form. Your name, email, and what you need, plus your company and phone number if you add them.
  • Free AI visibility scan. The business name, website, city and type of business you enter. If you also add your name or email, the scan result is saved with those details so I can follow up. If you leave them blank, the scan isn't saved as a lead.
  • Call requests. If you ask for a call from your scan results, your name, email, the times you suggest and your time zone are saved with your scan result so I can confirm a time.
  • AI advisor. The messages you type or speak are sent to my server and to an AI model to produce a reply, along with the page you're on and your browser's language setting. If you give the advisor your contact details, it saves them together with the conversation so I can follow up properly. If you ask for something none of the AI products it recommends can do yet, it saves a short summary of that request together with the conversation, even if you haven't given contact details, so I can consider building it. Please don't share sensitive personal details in the chat.
  • Email. If you email me, I receive whatever you send.
  • Technical logs. Like almost every website, the hosting provider (Vercel) automatically processes your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Visitor statistics (only if you allow it). If you turn on "Visitor statistics", Vercel Web Analytics counts the pages you visit, the site that sent you, and your country, browser, operating system and device type. It sets no cookies and doesn't identify you; I only see totals.
  • Scan and call request counts. When a scan finishes or a call request is sent, the server adds one to a count in Vercel Web Analytics, so I can see how many scans lead to a call request. That count includes no name, email or business details.

I don't ask for and don't want sensitive information such as passwords, payment details, government ID numbers, health information, or confidential details of a legal matter. Please don't send it.

What this site doesn't do

  • No advertising trackers, no analytics cookies, and no tracking across other websites.
  • No selling or renting of personal information, and no sharing it for targeted advertising.
  • No accounts, no payments, and no newsletter sign-ups.

How I use information

To reply to you, to prepare and deliver the services you asked about, to run and secure the site, and to keep basic business records. If you run a scan or message me, I may follow up about that request. I don't add you to a mailing list.

For visitors in places that require a legal basis (such as the EU and UK): I rely on your request and consent when you send me information, on taking steps toward a contract when you ask about services, and on my legitimate interest in running a secure website.

Cookies and browser storage

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It uses only the following:

  • Essential (always on). Your cookie choice is saved in your browser's local storage so you aren't asked on every page. If you open the AI advisor, the conversation is kept in your browser's session storage so it survives moving between pages; it is cleared when you close the tab. Neither is used to track you.
  • Embedded content (only if you allow it). Two portfolio pages show content hosted by other companies: JOUR 330 is hosted by Adobe Express and PR 351A by WordPress.com. Those companies may set their own cookies and will see your IP address when the content loads. Nothing from them loads until you choose "Accept all", turn on "Embedded content", or press "Allow and load" on that page.
  • Visitor statistics (only if you allow it). Counts page visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser; the script is served from this site. It only runs after you choose "Accept all" or turn on "Visitor statistics".

You can change your choice at any time: open cookie preferences (also linked at the bottom of every page). Choosing "Essential only" leaves the rest of the site working normally. Visitor statistics stay off unless you turn them on, and the site doesn't track you across other websites, so there is nothing extra to switch off for "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals.

Who else handles your information

  • Vercel hosts the site, stores submitted leads in private storage, routes AI requests through its AI Gateway, and runs Web Analytics (visitor statistics, if you allow them, and scan and call request counts).
  • AI model providers (such as Anthropic, OpenAI and Google, reached through Vercel AI Gateway) process AI advisor messages and scan details to generate replies.
  • Resend may be used to email me a notification when a new lead arrives (name, contact details and what you asked for).
  • Your browser's provider, only if you use voice in the AI advisor: speech recognition is done by your browser and may send audio to its provider (for example Google for Chrome or Apple for Safari). My site receives only the text.
  • Adobe and WordPress.com (Automattic), only if you allow embedded content, as described above.

These providers are in the United States or may process data there. I may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety or rights.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiries and saved conversations for as long as needed to respond and to work with you, and for a reasonable period afterwards for my records. Ask and I will delete yours sooner, unless I'm legally required to keep it. Hosting logs are kept by Vercel for a limited time under its own policies.

Your choices and rights

Email ryanjavaheri21@gmail.com to see, correct or delete the information I have about you, to get a copy of it, or to tell me to stop contacting you. I aim to respond within 30 days, and I will never treat you differently for asking.

  • California residents. I do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act, and I honor requests to know, correct and delete.
  • EU, UK and similar regions. You may also object to or restrict certain uses, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site isn't directed to children. The forms, scan and AI advisor are for adults 18 and over, and I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has sent me information, email me and I will delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and saved leads are kept in private, password-protected storage. No website can promise perfect security, which is another reason not to send sensitive information.

Changes

If the site starts collecting something new or working with a new provider, I will update this page and the date at the top before or when the change goes live.
